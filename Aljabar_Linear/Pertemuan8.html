Pertemuan8 (23/04/26)#

A.Hitung Determinan :

soal 1

\[\begin{split}A= \begin{bmatrix} -7 & -5 \\ 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

jawaban :

\[\begin{split}A= \begin{bmatrix} -7 & -5 \\ 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix} = -7 \cdot 4 - (-5) \cdot 1 = -28 - (-5) = -23 \end{split}\]

Determinannya adalah -23

soal 2

\[\begin{split}A= \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 & -3 \\ 1 & -2 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

jawaban :

Menggunakan ekspansi baris ke 3

\[ \sum_{j=1}^{n} (-1)^{3+j}a_{3j}det (A_{3j}) \]

kolom 1 :

\[\begin{split}+0 \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -3 \\ -2 & -1 \end{bmatrix} = 2 \cdot -1 - (-3) \cdot -2 = -2 - 6 = -8 -> 0 \cdot -8 = 0 \end{split}\]

kolom 2 :

\[\begin{split}-0 \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -3 \\ 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix} = 0 \cdot -1 - (-3) \cdot 1 = 0 - (-3) = 3 -> 0 \cdot 3 = 0 \end{split}\]

kolom 3 :

\[\begin{split}+1 \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 1 & -2 \end{bmatrix} = 0 \cdot -2 - 2 \cdot 1 = 0 - 2 = -2 \end{split}\]

hasil akhir :

\(0-0+(-2) = -2\)

Determinannya adalah -2

soal 3 :

\[\begin{split}A= \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 & 1 & 1\\ -3 & 1 & 1 & 1\\ 1 & 1 & -3 & 1\\ 1 & 1 & 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jawaban :

Menggunakan ekspansi baris ke 1

\[ \sum_{j=1}^{4} (-1)^{1+1}a_{11}det (A_{11}) \]
\[\begin{split} 1\begin{bmatrix}1 & 1 & 1 \\1 & -3 & 1 \\1 & 1 & -3\end{bmatrix} +3\begin{bmatrix}-3 & 1 & 1 \\1 & -3 & 1 \\1 & 1 & -3\end{bmatrix} +1 \begin{bmatrix}-3 & 1 & 1 \\1 & 1 & 1 \\1 & 1 & -3\end{bmatrix} -1\begin{bmatrix}-3 & 1 & 1 \\1 & 1 & -3 \\1 & 1 & 1\end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

setelah itu lanjut mencari pada matriks 3x3 nya :

\[\begin{split}\begin{aligned} D = \\ &\mathbf{+1} \begin{bmatrix} \begin{aligned} &\mathbf{+1} \left[ \begin{bmatrix} -3 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix} = (-3 \cdot -3) - (1 \cdot 1) = 8 \right] \\ &-1 \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix} = (1 \cdot -3) - (1 \cdot 1) = -4 \right] \\ &+1 \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = (1 \cdot 1) - (-3 \cdot 1) = 4 \right] \end{aligned} \end{bmatrix} \\ \\ &\mathbf{+3} \begin{bmatrix} \begin{aligned} &\mathbf{-3} \left[ \begin{bmatrix} -3 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix} = (-3 \cdot -3) - (1 \cdot 1) = 8 \right] \\ &-1 \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix} = (1 \cdot -3) - (1 \cdot 1) = -4 \right] \\ &+1 \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = (1 \cdot 1) - (-3 \cdot 1) = 4 \right] \end{aligned} \end{bmatrix} \\ \\ &\mathbf{+1} \begin{bmatrix} \begin{aligned} &\mathbf{-3} \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix} = (1 \cdot -3) - (1 \cdot 1) = -4 \right] \\ &-1 \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix} = (1 \cdot -3) - (1 \cdot 1) = -4 \right] \\ &+1 \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = (1 \cdot 1) - (1 \cdot 1) = 0 \right] \end{aligned} \end{bmatrix} \\ \\ &\mathbf{-1} \begin{bmatrix} \begin{aligned} &\mathbf{-3} \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = (1 \cdot 1) - (-3 \cdot 1) = 4 \right] \\ &-1 \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = (1 \cdot 1) - (-3 \cdot 1) = 4 \right] \\ &+1 \left[ \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = (1 \cdot 1) - (1 \cdot 1) = 0 \right] \end{aligned} \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned}\end{split}\]

Hitung seluruh hasil :

\(+1 [ (+1 \cdot 8) + (-1 \cdot -4) + (+1 \cdot 4) ] = 1[8 + 4 + 4] = \mathbf{16}\)

\(+3 [ (-3 \cdot 8) + (-1 \cdot -4) + (+1 \cdot 4) ] = 3[-24 + 4 + 4] = 3[-16] = \mathbf{-48}\)

\(+1 [ (-3 \cdot -4) + (-1 \cdot -4) + (+1 \cdot 0) ] = 1[12 + 4 + 0] = \mathbf{16}\)

\(-1 [ (-3 \cdot 4) + (-1 \cdot 4) + (1 \cdot 0) ] = -1[-12 - 4 + 0] = \mathbf{16}\)

Hasil Akhir Determinan Utama (\(D\)): $\(D = 16 - 48 + 16 + 16 = \mathbf{0}\)$

Maka determinannya adalah 0

B.CARI INVERS

menggunakan rumus Adjoin:

\[(\operatorname{adj} A)_{ij} = (-1)^{i+j} M_{ji}\]

dan Invers :

\[A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det A} \operatorname{adj} A.\]

soal 4 :

Karna soalnya sama seperti tugas A, maka diketahui determinan dari soal 1 adalah -23

menghitung adjoin :

\(C_{11} = (-1)^{1+1} (4) = 4\)

\(C_{12} = (-1)^{1+2} (1) = -1\)

\(C_{21} = (-1)^{2+1} (-5) = 5\)

\(C_{22} = (-1)^{2+2} (-7) = -7\)

Maka diketahui kofaktor sebagai berikut :

\[\begin{split}A \begin{bmatrix} 4 & -1 \\ 5 & -7 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

kemudian dilakukan transpose, menjadi :

\[\begin{split}A = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ -1 & -7 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

hitung invers :

= \(A^{-1} = \frac{1}{-23} \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ -1 & -7 \end{bmatrix}\)

= \(A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} -\frac{4}{23} & -\frac{5}{23} \\ \frac{1}{23} & \frac{7}{23} \end{bmatrix}\)

maka inversnya adalah :

\(\begin{bmatrix} -\frac{4}{23} & -\frac{5}{23} \\ \frac{1}{23} & \frac{7}{23} \end{bmatrix}\)

soal 5

Pada soal sebelumnya, diketahui determinannya adalah -2

Menghitung adjoinnya

Baris 1

\(C_{11} = + \begin{vmatrix} -2 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -2 - 0 = \mathbf{-2}\)

\(C_{12} = - \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -(1 - 0) = \mathbf{-1}\)

\(C_{13} = + \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = 0 - 0 = \mathbf{0}\)

Baris 2

\(C_{21} = - \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -3 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -(2 - 0) = \mathbf{-2}\)

\(C_{22} = + \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -3 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 0 - 0 = \mathbf{0}\)

\(C_{23} = - \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = 0 - 0 = \mathbf{0}\)

Baris 3

\(C_{31} = + \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -3 \\ -2 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = -2 - 6 = \mathbf{-8}\)

\(C_{32} = - \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -3 \\ 1 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = -(0 - (-3)) = \mathbf{-3}\)

\(C_{33} = + \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 1 & -2 \end{vmatrix} = 0 - 2 = \mathbf{-2}\)

Hasilnya adalah :

\[\begin{split}A \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -1 & 0 \\ -2 & 0 & 0 \\ -8 & -3 & -2 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

kemudian di transpose untuk mendapatkan adjoin :

\[\begin{split}\operatorname{adj}(A) = \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -2 & -8 \\ -1 & 0 & -3 \\ 0 & 0 & -2 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Hitung Invers:

\[\begin{split}A^{-1} = \frac{1}{-2} \begin{bmatrix} -2 & -2 & -8 \\ -1 & 0 & -3 \\ 0 & 0 & -2 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]
\[\begin{split}A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{-2}{-2} & \frac{-2}{-2} & \frac{-8}{-2} \\ \frac{-1}{-2} & \frac{0}{-2} & \frac{-3}{-2} \\ \frac{0}{-2} & \frac{0}{-2} & \frac{-2}{-2} \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

maka inversnya adalah :

\[\begin{split}\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 4 \\ \frac{1}{2} & 0 & \frac{3}{2} \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

soal 6

Karna determinan yang di temukan adalah 0, maka matriks ini tidak memiliki invers

penyebab :

\[A^{-1} = \frac{1}{0} \operatorname{adj}(A)\]

karna determinannya 0 maka pembagiannya akan menjadi tidak terdefinisi, sehingga inversnya tidak ada
