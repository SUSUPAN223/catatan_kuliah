TUGAS AKHIR#

A. Soal Determinan Matriks 3×3#

Soal 1#

\[\begin{split} A= \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jawaban :

Menggunakan ekspansi baris ke 1

\[\sum_{j=1}^{3} (-1)^{1+j}a_{1j}\det (A_{1j})\]

Kolom 1: $\(+2 \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 5 \\ 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = 2 \cdot (4 \cdot 1 - 5 \cdot 2) = 2 \cdot (4 - 10) = 2 \cdot (-6) = -12\)$

Kolom 2: $\(-1 \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 5 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = -1 \cdot (0 \cdot 1 - 5 \cdot 1) = -1 \cdot (0 - 5) = -1 \cdot (-5) = 5\)$

Kolom 3: $\(+3 \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 4 \\ 1 & 2 \end{bmatrix} = 3 \cdot (0 \cdot 2 - 4 \cdot 1) = 3 \cdot (0 - 4) = 3 \cdot (-4) = -12\)$

Hasil Akhir: $\(-12 + 5 + (-12) = -19\)$

Determinannya adalah -19.

Soal 2#

\[\begin{split}B= \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 4 \\ 2 & 5 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{split}\]

Jawaban :

Menggunakan ekspansi baris ke 1

Rumus Umum Ekspansi Kofaktor (Baris 1): $\(\sum_{j=1}^{3} (-1)^{1+j}b_{1j}\det (B_{1j})\)$

Kolom 1: $\(+3 \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 4 \\ 5 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = 3 \cdot (0 \cdot 1 - 4 \cdot 5) = 3 \cdot (0 - 20) = 3 \cdot (-20) = -60\)$

Kolom 2: $\(-2 \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 4 \\ 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = -2 \cdot (1 \cdot 1 - 4 \cdot 2) = -2 \cdot (1 - 8) = -2 \cdot (-7) = 14\)$

Kolom 3: $\(+1 \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 5 \end{bmatrix} = 1 \cdot (1 \cdot 5 - 0 \cdot 2) = 1 \cdot (5 - 0) = 1 \cdot 5 = 5\)$

Hasil Akhir: $\(-60 + 14 + 5 = -41\)$

Determinannya adalah -41.

Soal 3#

\[\begin{split}C=\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 4 & 6 \\ 1 & 1 & 1\end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Jawaban:

Menggunakan ekspansi baris ke-1: $\(\sum_{j=1}^{3} (-1)^{1+j}c_{1j}\det (C_{1j})\)$

Kolom 1: $\(+1 \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 6 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = 1 \cdot (4 \cdot 1 - 6 \cdot 1) = 1 \cdot (4 - 6) = 1 \cdot (-2) = -2\)$

Kolom 2: $\(-2 \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 6 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = -2 \cdot (2 \cdot 1 - 6 \cdot 1) = -2 \cdot (2 - 6) = -2 \cdot (-4) = 8\)$

Kolom 3: $\(+3 \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 4 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = 3 \cdot (2 \cdot 1 - 4 \cdot 1) = 3 \cdot (2 - 4) = 3 \cdot (-2) = -6\)$

Hasil Akhir: $\(-2 + 8 + (-6) = 0\)$

Determinannya adalah 0.

B. Soal Dekomposisi Matriks (LU Decomposition)#

Soal 4#

\[\begin{split}A=\begin{bmatrix}2 & 4 & 2 \\ 1 & 5 & 2 \\ 1 & 2 & 4\end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Jawaban:

Kolom 1:

  • Pengali \(m_{21}\): $\(m_{21} = \frac{1}{2}\)$

  • Operasi Baris Elemen (OBE) \(B_2\): $\(B_2 \leftarrow B_2 - \frac{1}{2}B_1 = [1, 5, 2] - [1, 2, 1] = [0, 3, 1]\)$

  • Pengali \(m_{31}\): $\(m_{31} = \frac{1}{2}\)$

  • Operasi Baris Elemen (OBE) \(B_3\): $\(B_3 \leftarrow B_3 - \frac{1}{2}B_1 = [1, 2, 4] - [1, 2, 1] = [0, 0, 3]\)$

Kolom 2:

  • Pengali \(m_{32}\): $\(m_{32} = \frac{0}{3} = 0\)$

  • Operasi Baris Elemen (OBE) \(B_3\): $\(B_3 \leftarrow B_3 - 0 B_2 = [0, 0, 3] - [0, 0, 0] = [0, 0, 3]\)$

Hasil Akhir (Faktorisasi LU): $\(L = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ \frac{1}{2} & 1 & 0 \\ \frac{1}{2} & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad U = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 4 & 2 \\ 0 & 3 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}\)$

Soal 5#

\[\begin{split}B=\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 5 & 3 \\ 4 & 10 & 8\end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Jawaban:

Kolom 1:

  • Pengali \(m_{21}\): $\(m_{21} = \frac{2}{1} = 2\)$

  • Operasi Baris Elemen (OBE) \(B_2\): $\(B_2 \leftarrow B_2 - 2B_1 = [2, 5, 3] - [2, 4, 2] = [0, 1, 1]\)$

  • Pengali \(m_{31}\): $\(m_{31} = \frac{4}{1} = 4\)$

  • Operasi Baris Elemen (OBE) \(B_3\): $\(B_3 \leftarrow B_3 - 4B_1 = [4, 10, 8] - [4, 8, 4] = [0, 2, 4]\)$

Kolom 2:

  • Pengali \(m_{32}\): $\(m_{32} = \frac{2}{1} = 2\)$

  • Operasi Baris Elemen (OBE) \(B_3\): $\(B_3 \leftarrow B_3 - 2B_2 = [0, 2, 4] - [0, 2, 2] = [0, 0, 2]\)$

Hasil Akhir (Faktorisasi LU): $\(L = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 4 & 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad U = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}\)$

Soal 6#

\[\begin{split}C=\begin{bmatrix}4 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & 5 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 3\end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Jawaban:

Kolom 1:

  • Pengali \(m_{21}\): $\(m_{21} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}\)$

  • Operasi Baris Elemen (OBE) \(B_2\): $\(B_2 \leftarrow B_2 - \frac{1}{2}B_1 = [2, 5, 1] - [2, 1, 0] = [0, 4, 1]\)$

  • Pengali \(m_{31}\): $\(m_{31} = \frac{0}{4} = 0\)$

  • Operasi Baris Elemen (OBE) \(B_3\): $\(B_3 \leftarrow B_3 - 0B_1 = [0, 1, 3] - [0, 0, 0] = [0, 1, 3]\)$

Kolom 2:

  • Pengali \(m_{32}\): $\(m_{32} = \frac{1}{4}\)$

  • Operasi Baris Elemen (OBE) \(B_3\): $\(B_3 \leftarrow B_3 - \frac{1}{4}B_2 = [0, 1, 3] - \left[0, 1, \frac{1}{4}\right] = \left[0, 0, \frac{11}{4}\right]\)$

Hasil Akhir (Faktorisasi LU): $\(L = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ \frac{1}{2} & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & \frac{1}{4} & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad U = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 4 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & \frac{11}{4} \end{bmatrix}\)$

C. Soal Invers Matriks 3×3#

Soal 7#

\[\begin{split}A=\begin{bmatrix}1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 & 4\end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Jawaban:

1. Menghitung Determinan (Ekspansi Baris 1)#

\[\begin{split}\det(A) = 1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 3 & 4 \end{vmatrix} - 2 \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 4 \end{vmatrix} + 1 \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix}\end{split}\]
\[\det(A) = 1(4 - 3) - 2(0 - 2) + 1(0 - 2) = 1(1) - 2(-2) + 1(-2) = 1 + 4 - 2 = 3\]

2. Menghitung Kofaktor#

  • Baris 1: $\(C_{11} = + \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 3 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = 4 - 3 = 1\)\( \)\(C_{12} = - \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = -(0 - 2) = 2\)\( \)\(C_{13} = + \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 0 - 2 = -2\)$

  • Baris 2: $\(C_{21} = - \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 3 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = -(8 - 3) = -5\)\( \)\(C_{22} = + \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = 4 - 2 = 2\)\( \)\(C_{23} = - \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = -(3 - 4) = 1\)$

  • Baris 3: $\(C_{31} = + \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 2 - 1 = 1\)\( \)\(C_{32} = - \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -(1 - 0) = -1\)\( \)\(C_{33} = + \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 1 - 0 = 1\)$

3. Matriks Kofaktor dan Adjoin (Transpose)#

\[\begin{split}\text{Kofaktor} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & -2 \\ -5 & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \implies \operatorname{adj}(A) = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -5 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 & -1 \\ -2 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

4. Menghitung Invers#

\[\begin{split}A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det A} \operatorname{adj}(A) = \frac{1}{3} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -5 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 & -1 \\ -2 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Hasil Akhir: $\(A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{3} & -\frac{5}{3} & \frac{1}{3} \\ \frac{2}{3} & \frac{2}{3} & -\frac{1}{3} \\ -\frac{2}{3} & \frac{1}{3} & \frac{1}{3} \end{bmatrix}\)$

Soal 8#

\[\begin{split}B=\begin{bmatrix}2 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 2\end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Jawaban:

1. Menghitung Determinan (Ekspansi Baris 1)#

\[\begin{split}\det(B) = 2 \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} - 1 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{vmatrix} + 0 \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix}\end{split}\]
\[\det(B) = 2(4 - 1) - 1(2 - 0) + 0 = 2(3) - 2 = 6 - 2 = 4\]

2. Menghitung Kofaktor#

  • Baris 1: $\(C_{11} = + \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 4 - 1 = 3\)\( \)\(C_{12} = - \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = -(2 - 0) = -2\)\( \)\(C_{13} = + \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 1 - 0 = 1\)$

  • Baris 2: $\(C_{21} = - \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = -(2 - 0) = -2\)\( \)\(C_{22} = + \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 4 - 0 = 4\)\( \)\(C_{23} = - \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -(2 - 0) = -2\)$

  • Baris 3: $\(C_{31} = + \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 1 - 0 = 1\)\( \)\(C_{32} = - \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -(2 - 0) = -2\)\( \)\(C_{33} = + \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = 4 - 1 = 3\)$

3. Matriks Kofaktor dan Adjoin (Transpose)#

\[\begin{split}\text{Kofaktor} = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -2 & 1 \\ -2 & 4 & -2 \\ 1 & -2 & 3 \end{bmatrix} \implies \operatorname{adj}(B) = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -2 & 1 \\ -2 & 4 & -2 \\ 1 & -2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

4. Menghitung Invers#

\[\begin{split}B^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det B} \operatorname{adj}(B) = \frac{1}{4} \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -2 & 1 \\ -2 & 4 & -2 \\ 1 & -2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Hasil Akhir: $\(B^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{3}{4} & -\frac{1}{2} & \frac{1}{4} \\ -\frac{1}{2} & 1 & -\frac{1}{2} \\ \frac{1}{4} & -\frac{1}{2} & \frac{3}{4} \end{bmatrix}\)$

Soal 9#

\[\begin{split}C=\begin{bmatrix}3 & 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 0 & -2 \\ 0 & 1 & 1\end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Jawaban:

1. Menghitung Determinan (Ekspansi Baris 1)#

\[\begin{split}\det(C) = 3 \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} - 0 + 2 \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix}\end{split}\]
\[\det(C) = 3(0 - (-2)) + 2(2 - 0) = 3(2) + 2(2) = 6 + 4 = 10\]

2. Menghitung Kofaktor#

  • Baris 1: $\(C_{11} = + \begin{vmatrix} 0 & -2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 0 - (-2) = 2\)\( \)\(C_{12} = - \begin{vmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -(2 - 0) = -2\)\( \)\(C_{13} = + \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 2 - 0 = 2\)$

  • Baris 2: $\(C_{21} = - \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -(0 - 2) = 2\)\( \)\(C_{22} = + \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 3 - 0 = 3\)\( \)\(C_{23} = - \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = -(3 - 0) = -3\)$

  • Baris 3: $\(C_{31} = + \begin{vmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 0 & -2 \end{vmatrix} = 0 - 0 = 0\)\( \)\(C_{32} = - \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 2 \\ 2 & -2 \end{vmatrix} = -(-6 - 4) = 10\)\( \)\(C_{33} = + \begin{vmatrix} 3 & 0 \\ 2 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = 0 - 0 = 0\)$

3. Matriks Kofaktor dan Adjoin (Transpose)#

\[\begin{split}\text{Kofaktor} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -2 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 & -3 \\ 0 & 10 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \implies \operatorname{adj}(C) = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 2 & 0 \\ -2 & 3 & 10 \\ 2 & -3 & 0 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

4. Menghitung Invers#

\[\begin{split}C^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det C} \operatorname{adj}(C) = \frac{1}{10} \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 2 & 0 \\ -2 & 3 & 10 \\ 2 & -3 & 0 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Hasil Akhir: $\(C^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{5} & \frac{1}{5} & 0 \\ -\frac{1}{5} & \frac{3}{10} & 1 \\ \frac{1}{5} & -\frac{3}{10} & 0 \end{bmatrix}\)$
